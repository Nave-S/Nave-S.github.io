SQNZ Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Overview

SQNZ is an interval timer for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. This policy explains what data is processed, on what legal basis, how long it is kept, and what rights you have. The short version: the app itself sends nothing to any server. Only if you write to us does your email reach us.

Do You Have to Provide Anything?

No. SQNZ has no account, no registration, no login and no required field. You are not asked for a name, an email address or a date of birth, and nothing you leave empty stops the app from working. Everything you create (timers, flows, statistics) you create because you want to, and it stays on your devices. The only features that ask you for anything are optional and described below: saving to Apple Health, creating a timer from text with your own AI app, sharing a timer or flow with your notes, and writing to us.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Timer configurations: your created timers, flows, and settings
  • Workout statistics: workout history, streaks, and duration
  • App preferences: settings, language, and sound

Data We Do Not Collect

  • The app never asks for your name, email or address and sends us nothing. We only see your email if you write to us (see "If You Write to Us")
  • No location tracking
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No microphone or voice recordings
  • No cloud sync of our own: the app transfers nothing by itself
  • No selling or sharing of data

Creating a Timer with an AI App

The import uses no AI of its own and makes no request to any AI service. What the import does is this: it takes the description you typed, wraps it in a prepared instruction that asks for our timer format, copies the whole thing to your clipboard, and opens the AI app you picked: ChatGPT or Claude, or their website if the app is not installed. From that point on you are in someone else's app, under your own account. Your description and anything the AI writes back are processed there under that provider's privacy policy and terms, not ours. We never see the exchange. When you paste the answer back into SQNZ, it becomes a timer on your device like any other. If you would rather not use this, you do not have to: every timer can be built by hand, and the app is fully usable without the import.

Sharing Timers and Flows

When you share a timer or flow, SQNZ creates a file with its structure: its name, phases, durations and settings. Your notes go into it only if you agree when you share. The file contains no name of yours, no message and no time stamp. You send it yourself, by the route you choose (for example AirDrop, Messages or Mail); that route's provider handles the transfer under its own terms. We receive nothing of it. Whoever gets the file can open it in SQNZ and add the timer or flow to their library. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract, expected feature).

Data Processors

Our only processor is our email provider, and only for emails you send us yourself (see "If You Write to Us"). The app itself uses no external data processors acting on its behalf: it transmits no timer, workout, or preference data to any server and uses no analytics or tracking SDKs. Everything you create stays on your devices. The only data flows that exist are operated by Apple as an independent controller (app distribution, in-app purchases, optional system crash reports and syncing with your Apple Watch) and are described in the sections below. If you have turned on iCloud Backup or Health in iCloud in iOS, Apple stores this data in your iCloud account. We have no access to it. Something leaves your devices only when you share it yourself, write to us or hand a plan to your AI app.

Apple Watch Sync

If you pair SQNZ with an Apple Watch, your timers, favorites and workout statistics sync between iPhone and the paired Watch via Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. The transfer runs over Apple's connection between iPhone and watch; no server of ours is involved. Apple chooses the route. No data is stored on Apple servers beyond the temporary delivery buffer the system uses. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract, expected feature).

Apple Health (optional)

SQNZ can, if you choose, save completed timer sessions to Apple Health on your device, the same whether the timer ran on iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch: as a workout (activity type, start, end, duration). You choose for each timer whether and in which category it is saved; timers without a Health category and sessions shorter than one minute are never saved. This feature is off by default.

  • SQNZ does not read any data from Apple Health and does not display any health data.
  • The data written stays in Apple Health on your device. SQNZ never transmits it anywhere: not to us, not to third parties, not to any server; we have no access at any time. No analysis, no advertising, no profiling.
  • Entries written by SQNZ remain in Apple Health, even after you uninstall the app, until you delete them there. You control the retention period yourself.
  • The entries written are health data (special category, Art. 9(1) GDPR). Data is saved only if you actively turn the feature on and give your explicit consent in the Apple Health system dialog (Art. 9(2)(a) in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Voluntary: the timer works without it as well. Without this feature, the Apple Watch does not vibrate while your arm is lowered.
  • Withdraw at any time with effect for the future: turn the feature off in SQNZ settings or revoke the permission under "Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → SQNZ". You can remove entries already stored in Apple Health, or via SQNZ ("delete all sessions saved by SQNZ", while write permission still exists). The lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal remains unaffected.
  • Apple Health may synchronize your data across devices via iCloud; in that case it travels to Apple's servers. This is an Apple system service that you turn on or off yourself in iOS and that Apple is responsible for.
  • Since we receive no copy of this data, you exercise your rights to it directly via Apple Health. Your other rights (access, complaint to the LDI NRW, etc.): see the "Your Rights (GDPR)" section. The controller is named in the "Controller / Responsible Person" section.

Workout Sessions on Apple Watch (optional)

When you start a timer on your Apple Watch that is set to a sport Health category and have Apple Health saving enabled, SQNZ starts a workout session on the Watch. This is what allows the Watch to alert you with a haptic tap while your wrist is lowered; without a workout session, watchOS suppresses those taps entirely. Whether a timer counts as a sport timer is determined by the Health category you have assigned to it (see previous section). Timers without a sport Health category (learning timers, for example) never start a workout session.

  • While a workout session runs, your Apple Watch records heart rate and active energy on its own and saves them to Apple Health on your device. This recording is performed by watchOS, not by SQNZ, but it happens because SQNZ starts the session, so we name it here.
  • SQNZ has no access to these values. We request no read permission of any kind, we run no queries, and we never receive, store or transmit heart rate or energy data. Our statement above stands unchanged: SQNZ does not read any data from Apple Health.
  • The recorded data stays in Apple Health on your device, under your control. There are no recipients on our side: not us, not third parties, no server of ours. Apple Health's optional iCloud sync described in the previous section applies to this data in the same way.
  • This data is health data (special category, Art. 9(1) GDPR). It is recorded only if you have actively enabled Apple Health saving in SQNZ (the notice you confirm when turning it on describes this recording) and allowed workout saving in the Apple Health system dialog (Art. 9(2)(a) in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Voluntary: the timer works without it as well. Without this feature, the Apple Watch does not vibrate while your arm is lowered.
  • Withdraw at any time with effect for the future: turn Apple Health saving off in SQNZ settings, or revoke the permission under "Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → SQNZ". No further workout session will be started. Heart rate and energy entries already recorded by watchOS can be deleted directly in Apple Health; because SQNZ did not write them, SQNZ's "delete all sessions saved by SQNZ" function does not remove them. The lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal remains unaffected.
  • Your Watch also counts this activity toward your Activity Rings. That is a system function of watchOS, not a feature of SQNZ.

Children

SQNZ is not directed at children under 13. It asks for no age and has no way to verify one, because it has no account and collects nothing about you. The Apple Health feature is the one part of SQNZ that processes health data, and it is off by default and requires an explicit decision to turn on. If you are under 16, turn it on only together with a parent or guardian; the Health notice in the app says so as well. If you are a parent and want the app used without it, leave that switch off.

Phone Call Detection (iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch)

The app observes whether a phone call is currently active via Apple's CallKit framework, so a running timer can auto-pause when you take a call. No call metadata (caller, number, duration) is read, stored, transmitted or logged; only the on/off signal is held in memory for the duration of the running timer. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract, expected feature). This pause is always on; there is no setting to turn it off.

Notifications

SQNZ uses local notifications on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch to alert you when a timer ends, even if the screen is off or the app is in the background. On iPhone, a notification can also mark a phase change while the app is in the background. iOS asks for your permission once. On iPhone, SQNZ waits until you have completed a workout, explains what the notifications are for, and shows the iOS dialog only if you agree; as long as iOS has not asked yet, you can also allow notifications under Settings → Sounds & Haptics. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings. No push tokens are registered with any remote server; all notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract, expected feature), and your permission in the iOS system dialog.

Voice Announcements

Spoken phase names and countdowns use Apple's on-device speech synthesis (AVSpeechSynthesizer). No audio is recorded, transmitted, or sent to Apple servers. Apple's higher-quality voices (Enhanced or Premium quality) that you may have downloaded via iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content remain entirely on your device. If you choose the optional AI voice (Supertonic 3) instead, the announcements are generated by an AI model that ships inside the app and runs only on your device. The generated clips are stored inside the app on your device, excluded from device backups, carry a machine-readable marking that they are AI-generated, and are never transmitted. SQNZ records locally which version of the AI voice rules of use you accepted.

iCloud Backup

SQNZ stores its data in the standard iOS app sandbox. If you have iCloud device backup enabled in iOS Settings, your timer configurations and workout statistics may be included in that backup. Apple encrypts iCloud backups end-to-end when Advanced Data Protection is on. The app itself does not write to iCloud; there is no proprietary cloud sync.

In-App Purchases

SQNZ offers one optional in-app purchase, the Premium unlock. It is a one-time purchase, handled entirely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. SQNZ has no access to your payment information, sees no name, no card and no Apple Account, and stores nothing about the transaction. What the app does keep is a single yes/no flag recording that Premium is unlocked, so the unlock is not lost. Where that flag is stored, and how long it stays, is described under "Storage Duration". Payment data is processed by Apple as an independent controller. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract).

Records of Your Consent

Where the app asks you to confirm something, it notes on the device that you did, with the date and which version of the text you saw. This applies to the Apple Health notice, the health disclaimer before your first timer, and the hearing-protection warning. We are required to be able to show that consent was given (Art. 7(1) GDPR), and these notes are how the app does that. One further note is not a consent but an agreement: when you switch on the AI voice, the app records which version of its rules of use you agreed to, when, and in which language, so that both sides can see what was agreed (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of the contract). They all stay on your device; we never see them and they are never transmitted. All of them are cleared by "Reset everything" under Settings → Data; "Reset settings to defaults" clears the AI voice agreement and the hearing-protection note; the hearing-protection one is also described under "Your Rights (GDPR)". Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation).

Distribution and Apple

SQNZ is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect diagnostic, usage, and download metadata independently from this app under Apple's own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). SQNZ has no access to that data and is not the controller in this respect. In App Store Connect, Apple shows us aggregated figures: downloads and purchases, and, only from users who allowed sharing with app developers, also sessions, active devices and crashes. These figures do not tell us who you are.

International Data Transfers

The app itself transfers nothing to a third country. The only transfers that exist are Apple's own, and SQNZ is not involved in them. Apple Inc. is based in the United States. According to Apple's own privacy policy (as of September 14, 2026), transfers of personal data collected in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are governed by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR, Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914). Apple provides a copy of those clauses on request. Since SQNZ has no access to that data flow, Apple acts as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.

Storage Duration

Your data remains on your devices until you delete it manually or uninstall the app. There is no automatic deletion period. SQNZ sends nothing to us or to any server; syncing with your Apple Watch is handled by Apple directly between your devices. Two entries are the exception, because they live in this device's keychain and survive an uninstall: the record that you acknowledged the hearing-protection warning, and the flag that Premium is unlocked. Both are described under "Your Rights (GDPR)". Neither is synced to iCloud. Statistics, timers and flows accumulate over time and stay on your devices. For emails to us, the deadlines under "If You Write to Us" apply.

If You Write to Us

SQNZ sends us nothing. Something from you reaches us only if you email us yourself at support@casystudio.com, for example with a question or a bug. Then we read what the email contains: your email address, your name if your mail app sends it, your text and any files you attach. We use it only for your request: to answer you and to sort out what you ask us for. There is no other way to answer you, because without your email address we cannot reach you. You never have to write to us; the app works without it. This section applies in the same way to phone calls to the number in the Legal Notice.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If your request concerns a contract with us, it is also Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. For files you attach, such as photos or screenshots, it is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): you give it by attaching the file and sending the email. You can withdraw it at any time by email. We then delete the files straight away; what happened before stays lawful.

We delete your email once your request is settled, at the latest 90 days afterwards. This also applies to our replies to you. We keep it longer only if a law requires us to. We delete attached files as soon as we no longer need them, at the latest 30 days after they arrive, unless you expressly allow us to keep them longer.

Your email is received and stored by our email provider, which is based in Germany. It acts as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR; processing takes place within the EU, and no transfer to third countries is envisaged. We pass it on to no one else, unless a law requires us to. On its way to us, your email also passes through your own email provider, under that provider's terms; we have no influence on that.

Many apps build in analytics tools that record how you use the app. SQNZ does not. An email to us is something different: it exists only because you write it, and we see only what is in it.

"Report a problem" and "Send feedback" only open an email draft to support@casystudio.com; the app itself sends nothing. For "Report a problem", the draft contains the sentence you put together by tapping the suggestions, your optional text, and a short line with your choices, the app language, the app version and build number. "Send feedback" only puts the app version in the subject line. Device details such as the device model or iOS version are not included. You see everything before it goes out and can change it; nothing is sent until you tap Send. If no mail app is set up, the app puts the text of a problem report on the clipboard, and you decide where to paste it.

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under Art. 15–22 GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your personal data, and to receive it in a portable form. Because everything stays on your device, you exercise most of these rights directly in the app. Timers, flows and workout history are deleted under Settings → Data ('Delete all timers', 'Delete all flows', 'Clear workout history'), and any timer can be exported as a file to take with you. Uninstalling removes the app's entire sandbox. Two entries sit outside it, in this device's keychain, and both survive an uninstall. Neither ever leaves this device; they are not synced to iCloud. (1) The record, with its date, that you acknowledged the hearing-protection warning. It is kept on purpose so the acknowledgement is not lost, and it is erased by 'Reset settings to defaults' under Settings → Data. (2) A yes/no flag that Premium is unlocked, kept so a purchase you paid for is not lost. This flag has no delete button in the app today; write to us and we will tell you how to remove it. For questions, and for any request under these rights, contact support@casystudio.com.

Right to Object & Complaint

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): you may object at any time to any processing based on legitimate interests. We process data on that legal basis only for emails you send us (see "If You Write to Us"). Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77 GDPR): you may complain to a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement. The authority competent for the controller is: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW), Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany.

Automated Decision-Making

There is none. SQNZ makes no automated decisions about you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR and does no profiling. The app counts down, keeps a history of what you finished, and shows you streaks. It draws no conclusions about you from that, and nothing it shows has any legal or similarly significant effect.

System Crash Reports

iOS itself may collect crash reports and share them with Apple and with us, the developer, via App Store Connect. This is a system-level process: SQNZ does not implement its own crash reporting, and we receive nothing unless you have allowed it in iOS. You can turn it off at any time under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share with App Developers. What we see is technical: the version, the device model, and where in the code the app stopped. Reports contain nothing you entered in SQNZ (no timers, no statistics, no health data), and we use them only to fix crashes. Apple decides what these reports contain and is the controller for collecting them.

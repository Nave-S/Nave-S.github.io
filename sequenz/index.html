Free and Premium

Free: 3 timers and 1 flow of your own, running all bundled templates, and weekly statistics. With Premium: unlimited timers and flows, editing and duplicating templates, month, year and records in statistics, and the Apple Watch app. Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Build your own timers

Set up intervals, rounds and breaks yourself, each phase with its own duration. Ready-made templates such as Tabata Classic are included. You can pass timers and flows on to others as a file (.sqtimer, .sqflow).

Flows

Chain several timers into one unit, with rests in between if you like. In this version, flows run on iPhone and iPad.

Sound, voice, haptics

A sound tells you when to switch; on iPhone, a vibration does too. SQNZ announces phase names and countdowns; you can switch this off in Settings. Instead of the Apple voice you can choose an AI voice generated on your device. Your music keeps playing and only dips while an announcement plays.

Even with the screen locked

The timer keeps counting in the background, and on iPhone the Live Activity shows the countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Sound and voice cues keep playing while the device is locked. If the next cue is far off, SQNZ sends a notification instead. There are also widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, on iPhone also for StandBy, plus Control Center, Siri and Shortcuts.

Apple Watch

Start timers right on your wrist, or follow a timer you started on your iPhone. A run on the watch continues if your iPhone is out of range, and the watch taps your wrist at every switch, with your arm lowered only if saving to Apple Health is on. SQNZ syncs timers, favorites and statistics through Apple's connection between iPhone and watch, without any server of ours; flows stay on iPhone. There are complications for your watch face. The watch app requires Premium.

Statistics

SQNZ records every workout automatically, including the time of one you end early. It counts the time you actually trained: pauses, skipped parts and runs under one minute are left out. Your week and your streak are free; month, year and records come with Premium.

Create a timer from text

Describe your workout in your own words. Tap your AI app, for example ChatGPT or Claude, and SQNZ copies the request with your description and opens the app or its website. For another AI app, tap “Copy”. Paste the answer into SQNZ and check the preview before you save the timer. SQNZ uses no AI of its own for this and sends nothing. The app does not check whether the plan suits you: review every phase before you start.

Apple Health, if you want

When you finish a timer, SQNZ can save it to Apple Health on your device: sporty timers as a workout. The feature is off by default. SQNZ only writes to Apple Health and never reads your health data.

Privacy

SQNZ has no account, no servers of its own and no analytics or tracking SDKs. The app stores your timers and statistics on your device. What goes through Apple (purchase, sync with the watch, iCloud backup, Apple Health) and what happens when you create a timer from text is explained in the privacy policy.

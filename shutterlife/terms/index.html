Description

ShutterLife is a camera shutter count reader for photographers. It reads EXIF data from camera images (RAW and JPEG) to display the number of shutter actuations and, where the manufacturer states one, its tested minimum threshold next to it. Several photos can be compared at once (purchase check). Available for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Use of the App

  • The app is intended for personal and professional use by photographers
  • You may import camera images, your own or ones someone sent you, to read and compare shutter count data

Accuracy Disclaimer

Shutter count readings are extracted from image EXIF metadata and camera manufacturer data. While we strive for accuracy, readings may vary depending on camera model, firmware version, and image format. Where the manufacturer's tested minimum threshold for the shutter is shown, it is not a prediction of remaining life: shutters typically run well beyond the tested threshold. The purchase check only compares what the files say; it cannot establish whether the photos came from a particular camera. ShutterLife does not guarantee the accuracy of any reading or estimate.

Data Processing

ShutterLife operates entirely offline. The app transmits no data on its own and stores nothing on external servers; all processing happens on your device. If you email us support requests or photos yourself, the Privacy Policy applies to those.

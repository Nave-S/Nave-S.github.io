Check before you buy

ShutterLife can only show the shutter count if your camera writes it into the image file. These cannot be read: DSLRs from Canon and Sony (for example the EOS 5D Mark IV or the Alpha 68), smartphones, most compact cameras, and the Leica M series. They don’t store the count in the photo. Some mirrorless models are covered, the Canon EOS R6 for instance. Only the model list in the app decides: if your model is not on it, we have not tested it. With Nikon, Pentax and Fujifilm it sometimes works anyway; with Canon and Sony, rely on the list only. Readable formats are JPG, HEIC, HEIF, HIF, CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, PEF and DNG.

Your camera is missing?

Email us two original photos taken one right after the other at support@casystudio.com. JPEG is fine, do not send them through a messenger. Only the difference between them shows how your camera counts. We’ll then check whether the shutter count can be read. We use the photos only for this check and delete them within 30 days. More in the privacy policy.

How it works

Import a photo straight from your photo library or the Files app on iPhone and iPad. On a Mac, drag a photo into the window or open it from a folder or from Photos. ShutterLife reads the file and instantly shows you the shutter count. Important: the photo must be in its original format. Messaging apps like WhatsApp strip the capture data (EXIF), and the count with it, when sending. Please have the photo shared via AirDrop, email (original size), or a cloud service.

Count and tested threshold

See your shutter count and, next to it, how it relates to the threshold the manufacturer tested for. That threshold is a tested minimum, not a failure forecast: above it the manufacturer simply stopped testing. The figure appears when a confirmed manufacturer rating exists and the counter really tracks actuations. Fujifilm counters track frames instead, so those models show the count without a percentage.

Carefully tested

ShutterLife was built and tested on real camera files and shows the value your camera wrote into the image file. On the Canon EOS RP and R7 in HDR mode, the app adds the measured difference and marks it. Firmware updates or counter resets may affect the value shown. No software can guarantee that a file comes unaltered from the camera in question: if in doubt, compare the serial number in the report with the camera.

Privacy first

All processing happens on your device. The app uploads nothing, analyzes nothing, and does not track you. No internet connection required. We only see a photo if you choose to email one to us yourself.

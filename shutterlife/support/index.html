Why doesn't my camera show a shutter count?

Not every camera writes the shutter count into the image. These cannot be read: Canon DSLRs (for example the EOS 5D Mark IV), the Sony ZV-E1, smartphones, most compact cameras and the Leica M series. The list above is what counts. If your model is on it and no number appears, check two things: is it the original (not sent through WhatsApp or another messenger)? And does your camera run a firmware version we haven’t been able to test yet? ShutterLife then shows no number rather than a wrong one. Send us two photos with “Report a Camera” under “About”, and we’ll add your version.

How accurate is the shutter count?

ShutterLife shows the value your camera wrote into the image file. On the Canon EOS RP and R7 in HDR mode, the app adds the measured difference and marks it. Firmware updates or counter resets can affect the value. Where one exists, the manufacturer’s tested threshold appears next to it as a figure: a tested minimum, not a prediction of remaining life. Not every model has such a figure.

What does the purchase check look at?

When you open several original photos at once (up to 20), ShutterLife compares what the files say: same model, same serial number, no newer photo with a lower count, no editing software noted. If a file has no serial number, the app tells you that only the model can be compared. No software can guarantee that the photos came from the camera for sale: if in doubt, compare the serial number with the camera.

Which file formats are supported?

ShutterLife supports CR2, CR3 (Canon), NEF (Nikon), ARW (Sony), RAF (Fujifilm), PEF (Pentax), DNG (Leica, Ricoh), HEIC, HEIF, HIF, and JPEG files.

Does ShutterLife need an internet connection?

No. ShutterLife works 100% offline: the app sends nothing to any server, and all processing happens on your device. If you email us a support request or a photo yourself, our privacy policy covers that.

What does "Editing software recorded in this file" mean?

ShutterLife shows this message when the file's software field names an image editor such as Lightroom or Photoshop. For such files the app shows no shutter count, because editing software can overwrite or drop the original counter data. Use the unedited original straight from the camera.

How do I delete my reading history?

Go to History in the app and use “Delete All” to remove all saved readings. To delete a single reading, swipe it left, or right-click it on a Mac.
